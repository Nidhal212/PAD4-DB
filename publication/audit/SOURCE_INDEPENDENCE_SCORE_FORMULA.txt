The source_independence_score quantifies the degree to which a compound's
bioactivity data derives from genuinely independent experimental sources.

Score mapping (from scripts/04_dedup/04b_add_independence_scores.py):

  source_list                                    score
  BindingDB + ChEMBL + PubChem (confirmatory)   0.3
  BindingDB + PubChem (confirmatory)             0.5
  BindingDB + ChEMBL                             0.6
  Any other two-source combination               0.7
  Single source only                             1.0

Logic: The three dominant source combinations (BindingDB + PubChem,
BindingDB + ChEMBL + PubChem) all share BindingDB as an aggregator
of PubChem bioassay data, reducing effective independence. Score 0.3
reflects the lowest independence (all three sources share PubChem origin).
Score 1.0 means the compound appears in only one database.

Interpretation threshold: score >= 0.6 identifies 528 'truly independent'
compounds (i.e., not primarily driven by PubChem-BindingDB co-curation).
